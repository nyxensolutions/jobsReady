# Content Calendar — Nov 2026 (Foundation, Week 1–4)

Companion to `social-media-playbook.md`. Cluster: **Okhla** (swap to Noida Sector 63 if
that is the first cluster — every `[CLUSTER]` and pincode below changes with it).

Pillars: **P1** Aaj ki vacancy · **P2** Salary sach · **P3** Fraud se bacho ·
**P4** Kaam ki baat · **P5** Humne kiya

**Seasonal note:** Diwali falls in the second week of November 2026 (around 8 Nov —
confirm the date before scheduling). Festive delivery and warehouse hiring peaks in the
two weeks *before* it and drops for about four days after. Week 1 and 2 are therefore
front-loaded with P1 delivery/warehouse jobs; the days immediately after Diwali shift to
P4 evergreen content, when nobody is job-hunting anyway.

Standing daily items, not repeated below:
- 8:00 am WhatsApp broadcast — 3 jobs + trust line
- 8:30 am and 6:30 pm Facebook group posts (text format, §6.4 of the playbook)
- 7:00 pm Facebook Page post
- 9:00 am and 7:00 pm Instagram stories (1 job card + 1 behind-the-scenes)

**Ads test spec (Wed 18)**
- Budget ₹300/day, one campaign, two ad sets (Okhla pincodes / Noida 63 pincodes).
- Creative: the single best-performing organic job card from weeks 1–2, unchanged.
- Objective: conversions → completed profile. Not traffic.
- Review Sat 21: any ad set above ₹50 per completed profile after ₹1,500 spent is off.

**Thu 19 · Employer proof (LinkedIn)**

> We put 60 verified jobs live in Okhla over the last three weeks. 41 of them had 10 or
> more applications within 48 hours.
>
> Three things we learned about hiring helpers and drivers in NCR:
> 1. Jobs posted before 9 am get roughly twice the applications of jobs posted after 6 pm.
> 2. Listings that name the exact area ("Okhla Phase 2", not "South Delhi") get far more
>    applications. People will not travel blind.
> 3. The single biggest drop-off is asking for a resume. Our seekers mostly do not have one,
>    and they should not need one for a packing job.
>
> Hiring in Okhla or Noida 63? Posting is free until 31 March 2027.
