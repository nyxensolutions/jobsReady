# Jobs24India — Social Media Playbook

Owner: social media manager · Version 1, 27 Oct 2026
Sits under `Jobs24India-Marketing-Strategy.pdf`. The strategy decides *where we are*
(Foundation phase, Nov–Dec 2026, one cluster: Okhla or Noida Sector 63). This decides
*what gets posted, by whom, and what counts as working*.

## 1. What social is for

Social is not a brand channel for us. It is a **seeker-acquisition and trust channel**,
measured by completed seeker profiles at under ₹50 each — the same cost target the
strategy sets.

Order of priority:

1. **WhatsApp** — the real product channel. Everything else exists to grow it.
2. **Facebook groups + Page** — where NCR blue-collar job seekers already search.
3. **Instagram + YouTube Shorts (Hindi reels)** — reach and credibility, slower payback.
4. **Meta ads** — paid amplification of whatever organic already works. Never first.
5. **Moj / ShareChat** — mirror of the reels, zero extra production cost.
6. **LinkedIn** — employer-side only, low frequency, founder's personal profile.

We do **not** open X/Twitter, Threads, Pinterest, or Snapchat. Our seeker does not
hire-search there and we cannot staff them.

## 2. Non-negotiable rules

Every single asset — post, reel, poster, ad, WhatsApp template — carries the trust line:

> **"Jobs24India kabhi job ke liye paise nahi maangta. Koi paise maange to humein batayein."**
> (Jobs24India never charges for jobs. Report anyone who asks for money.)

Other hard rules:

- **No fake jobs, ever.** Every job we post on social must be live on the site and from a
  verified employer. One screenshot of a dead link in a Facebook group costs us the group.
- **No salary inflation.** Post the salary the employer actually entered. "₹15,000–18,000"
  not "₹18,000+". Our whole wedge is that we are the honest one.
- **No individual's face or name without written consent**, including success stories.
  Use first name + role + area ("Rakesh, helper, Okhla Phase 2") and keep the consent
  message in the WhatsApp thread.
- **Reply to every comment asking "job chahiye"** within 24h with the WhatsApp link. That
  comment is a free lead; ignoring it is the single most expensive mistake on this channel.
- **One language per asset.** Hindi creative with Hindi caption, English with English.
  Hinglish only in reel voiceover, where people actually speak it.
- Hindi first. Default to Hindi for seekers, English for employers.

## 4. Content pillars

Five pillars. Every post is exactly one of them. The weekly mix is fixed so we never
drift into posting only job cards.

| # | Pillar | Share | Job to be done | Primary format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Aaj ki vacancy** (today's jobs) | 40% | Drive applications now | Job card, carousel, story |
| 2 | **Salary sach** (salary truth) | 20% | Rank on the question people actually ask | Reel, carousel, blog repurpose |
| 3 | **Fraud se bacho** (scam safety) | 15% | Own the trust position | Reel, poster, text post |
| 4 | **Kaam ki baat** (how to get hired) | 15% | Interview, resume, PF, documents | Reel, carousel |
| 5 | **Humne kiya** (proof) | 10% | Hires, employer stories, drive recaps | Photo, short video |

**Pillar 1 never exceeds 40%.** A feed that is only job cards reads as a spam page and
Facebook groups ban it.

## 5. Cadence

Built for one founder plus one part-time content person, not an agency. Weekly load:
**~6 reels, 10 feed posts, 14 stories, 7 WhatsApp sends, 5 group posts a day.**

| Channel | Frequency | Slot (IST) | Pillars |
|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp broadcast | daily | 8:00 am | 1, plus 3 on Fridays |
| Facebook groups (5–8 local groups) | 2× daily | 8:30 am, 6:30 pm | 1 mostly, 2 and 3 weekly |
| Facebook Page | 1× daily | 7:00 pm | all |
| Instagram feed/carousel | 4× week | Tue/Thu/Sat/Sun 7:30 pm | 2, 3, 4, 5 |
| Instagram + YT Shorts reels | 3× week | Mon/Wed/Fri 8:00 pm | 2, 3, 4 |
| Instagram stories | 2× day | 9:00 am, 7:00 pm | 1, 5 |
| Moj / ShareChat | mirror reels within 24h | — | 2, 3, 4 |
| LinkedIn (founder) | 2× week | Tue/Thu 11:00 am | employer-side |

Timing rationale: 8:00–9:00 am is commute and shift-change; 6:30–8:30 pm is after the
shift ends, when job searching actually happens. Do not post seeker content 12–4 pm.

### Weekly production rhythm

- **Monday** — plan week, pull 10 real jobs from the cluster for job cards, shoot 3 reels in one sitting.
- **Tuesday–Friday** — publish, reply to comments and DMs, log leads.
- **Saturday** — hiring-drive or proof content, employer outreach posts.
- **Sunday** — numbers into the tracker, decide what to cut.

Batch-shoot all reels in one Monday sitting. A founder shooting a reel daily stops in
week three; this is the most common failure mode for a founder-led channel.

Caption (Hindi, Facebook/Instagram):

> 💰 ₹16,000–18,000 महीना · फुल टाइम

### 6.2 Reel script (pillar 2/3) — 25–35 seconds

Shot on a phone, founder on camera, Hindi, burnt-in Hindi subtitles (85% watch muted).

```
0–3s   HOOK, on screen + spoken: "Okhla mein delivery boy ki salary kitni hai? Sach bata deta hoon."
3–12s  PROOF: three real listings on screen, salary visible. "Ye aaj ke jobs hain — 16, 17, aur 18 hazaar."
12–22s CATCH: "Lekin 18 hazaar wale mein apni bike chahiye. Petrol apna. Haath mein 14 bachega."
22–30s TRUST + CTA: "Hum salary wahi likhte hain jo company deti hai. Aur job ke liye paise kabhi nahi lete.
       Link bio mein — free hai."
```

Rules: hook in the first 2 seconds and it must contain the **place and the number**.
No intro, no "hi guys", no logo animation. Face to camera, daylight, one take.

### 6.3 Scam-safety post (pillar 3)

The highest-shared format we have — it gets forwarded in family WhatsApp groups.

> ⚠️ ये 5 बातें दिखें तो वो नौकरी नकली है
> 1. रजिस्ट्रेशन फीस माँगे
> 2. इंटरव्यू से पहले ही "सेलेक्ट" कर दे
> 3. कंपनी का पता न बताए
> 4. सिर्फ़ WhatsApp पर बात करे, ऑफिस न बुलाए
> 5. आधार या बैंक डिटेल पहले माँगे
>
> Jobs24India पर हर employer वेरिफाइड है, और नौकरी के लिए हम कभी पैसे नहीं लेते।
> किसी ने पैसे माँगे? हमें बताएं 👉 [WhatsApp]

### 6.4 Facebook group post (plain text, no link in the first comment-less version)

Groups throttle link posts. Post the job as text, put the link in the first comment.

> ओखला फेज 2 में वेयरहाउस पैकर चाहिए — 8 पोस्ट
> ₹14,000–16,000 + ओवरटाइम। 12वीं पास। उम्र 18–35।
> कोई चार्ज नहीं। कंपनी वेरिफाइड है।
> अप्लाई करना है तो कमेंट में "JOB" लिखें, मैं लिंक भेज देता हूँ।

Commenting "JOB" is deliberate: it lifts the post in the group's ranking and gives us a
DM opening that is not an unsolicited message.

## 7. Tracking

Every social link is tagged. Without this we cannot hit the "under ₹50 per completed
profile" target because we cannot tell which channel produced the profile.

```
?utm_source=instagram|facebook|whatsapp|youtube|moj|sharechat
&utm_medium=organic|paid|group|broadcast|bio
&utm_campaign=okhla-launch-nov26
&utm_content=<pillar>-<asset-id>      e.g. p3-fake-offer-reel
```

Keep bare `jobs24india.com` only on printed posters and WhatsApp templates, where a long
URL looks suspicious — use the cluster page there instead.

### Weekly numbers (every Sunday, one sheet)

| Metric | Why | Target by Dec 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Completed seeker profiles from social | the real goal | 900 of the 3,000 total |
| Cost per completed profile (paid only) | kill switch for ads | < ₹50 |
| WhatsApp list size, per cluster | compounding asset | 2,500 |
| Group post → comment "JOB" rate | tells us if the copy works | > 3% of group members reached |
| Reel saves + shares (not views) | forwarding is the growth loop | 50+ per reel |
| DM/comment reply time | leads go stale in a day | < 12 hours |
| Applications per social-sourced seeker | quality, not just signup | 3+ |

Views and follower count are **not** reported. They do not predict a hire.

## 8. Paid: Meta ads

Hold paid until an organic asset has already proven itself. Then put ₹300–600/day behind
it, exactly as the strategy budgets.

- **Objective:** lead → profile completion, not reach, not engagement.
- **Geo:** pincode radius around the cluster only — 110020/110025 (Okhla), 201301/201309
  (Noida 63). Never all-India; it buys junk profiles.
- **Audience:** 18–40, Hindi language, interests in delivery/logistics/retail employment.
  Broad beats narrow at this budget; let the pixel work.
- **Creative:** real job cards with real salaries. A designed "ad" underperforms a
  screenshot-looking job card in this category, consistently.
- **Rule:** any ad set above ₹50 per completed profile after 3 days and ₹1,500 spent gets
  switched off the same day. No "let it learn" past that.
- Retarget site visitors who did not finish the profile — cheapest conversion we have.

## 9. Community management

- WhatsApp and DMs: answer within 12 hours, always in the language asked.
- Standard answer to "job chahiye": ask city + category, send the cluster link, ask them
  to complete the profile. Never send a list of 20 jobs; send 3 good matches.
- Someone reports being asked for money: thank them, ask for a screenshot, take the
  employer down the same day, and post about it (no names). This is the best trust
  content we will ever get, and it is free.
- Negative comment about a job being filled or stale: reply publicly, fix the listing,
  say it is fixed. Never delete the comment.

## 11. Open decisions

These need the founder, and they change what gets published:

1. **Which cluster is first** — Okhla or Noida Sector 63. Every piece of copy here names a
   place; this must be settled before week 1.
2. **Which WhatsApp number is public** — the Interakt business number or a second one.
3. **Who is on camera.** Hindi reels need one recognisable face for the whole quarter.
   Swapping faces resets trust.
4. **Founding Employer badge artwork** — needed for employer-side posts from week 3.
